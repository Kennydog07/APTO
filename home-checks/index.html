The problem

Empty homes come with strict insurance conditions

When someone dies, moves into care, or a house sits empty between tenants or during a sale, normal home insurance usually stops covering it fully. Specialist unoccupied-property cover takes over, and it almost always comes with an inspection condition.

That usually means someone has to visit every 7 or 14 days, check the property inside and out, and keep a dated record. If you live hours away, work full time, or are abroad, that’s very hard to keep up, and a missed visit could affect a claim.

Sound familiar?

  • You’re the executor, but you live a long way from the house.
  • The insurer wants weekly or fortnightly inspections “by a responsible person”.
  • Neighbours are kind, but you don’t want to keep asking.
  • Winter is coming and you’re worried about burst pipes.
  • Probate could take months, and the house can’t just be left.

Every visit

A thorough, consistent check every time, written up so it makes sense to you and to your insurer.

Doors, windows, locks, roof line, gutters and garden, looking for damage or break-ins.

Interior walk-round

Every room checked for leaks, damp, pests, smells and anything out of place.

Heating & water

Boiler and frost setting checked, taps run, stopcock and visible pipework looked over.

Gas, electricity and water readings recorded and photographed for your utility accounts.

Post collection

Post picked up so it doesn’t pile up behind the door. We can list, scan or forward it.

Same-day photo report

Timestamped photos and notes emailed to you the same day as the visit.

Insurer-ready visit log

FAQ

Common questions

Will your reports satisfy my insurer?

Our reports are designed around typical unoccupied-home conditions: a dated record of each inside-and-outside inspection, with photos. Every policy is a little different, so send us your policy wording and we’ll match our checks to it. You stay responsible for your policy, so it’s always worth confirming with your insurer that a professional inspector is acceptable.

How do I get a key to you if I live far away?

Most people post a key by tracked, signed-for delivery, or ask the solicitor, estate agent or a family member to drop one off. We log it, give it a code tag, and keep it in a locked key cabinet.

What happens if you find a problem?

If we find something urgent, like a leak, a break-in or no heating in freezing weather, we’ll call you straight away and agree the next step. With your permission, we can turn off the water at the stopcock or let in a tradesperson you choose.

Can you let in clearance firms, trades or estate agents?

Yes. We can meet house-clearance firms, tradespeople, valuers and estate agents at the property and lock up afterwards, charged as an extra per visit.

Do I have to sign up for a long contract?

No. Plans run visit by visit, and you can cancel any time with notice, for example when the house is sold, let or occupied again.
