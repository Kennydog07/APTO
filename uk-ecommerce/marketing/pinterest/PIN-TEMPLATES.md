# Pinterest playbook

**Why Pinterest first:** home organisation is a high-intent Pinterest category, and pins keep driving traffic for months, unlike short-lived social posts.

## Setup (owner, ~45 minutes)
1. Create a free Pinterest **business** account using the store email. Claim the website (Pinterest gives you a meta tag; paste it in Shopify → Online Store → Preferences or the theme `<head>`).
2. Create boards: *Small Kitchen Storage*, *Renter-Friendly Organisation*, *Wardrobe & Bedroom Storage*, *Bathroom Organisation*, *Hallway Ideas*.
3. For automatic publishing (Workflow 10), apply for Pinterest API access (developers.pinterest.com) and create an access token with `pins:write` and `boards:read`. Store it **only** in n8n credentials ("Pinterest API (Bearer)"). Until then, Workflow 10 stays in test mode and you upload approved pins manually from the dashboard text.

## Pin formula (generated by Workflow 09 into the approval queue)
- **Title (≤100 chars):** `{Product} — {main benefit}`
- **Description (≤500 chars):** product + up to 3 supplier-verified benefits + "Delivered from UK stock — see the product page for delivery estimates and full specifications."
- **Link:** product URL with `utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign={ISO week}`
- **Keywords:** product name, small space storage, home organisation UK, renter friendly
- **Visual brief:** natural light, real UK home setting, 2:3 vertical (1000×1500), short text overlay of the main benefit. **Only your own or supplier-licensed photos.**

## Anti-spam rules (enforced in code)
- One pin per product per week (fingerprint de-duplication in `ops-service/src/marketing.js`).
- Max 5 pins per day (`PUBLISH_LIMITS`).
- Nothing publishes without your approval, and nothing publishes while *Marketing publication* is paused.
