# Short-video production kit

Workflow 09 generates a script per featured product each week (dashboard → Marketing). These rules apply to all video:

- **Only real footage** of the actual product (supplier-licensed or filmed yourself). No AI-generated demonstrations of product performance.
- **No invented capacities, timings or results.** Quote supplier specifications only.
- Format: 9:16 vertical, 20–30 s, captions burned in (most viewers watch muted).
- Low-cost production: phone + daylight + free editor (CapCut, or Canva — Canva is connected to this account if you want me to draft templates later, with approval).

## Template: "Small space, sorted" (25 s)
| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Cluttered cupboard (real) | "This cupboard was chaos" | — |
| 3–8 s | Place the product | Product name | "This is the {product}." |
| 8–18 s | Show 2–3 verified features | One feature per caption | Short factual lines |
| 18–23 s | Finished cupboard | "Small spaces, sorted." | — |
| 23–25 s | Logo / URL | "Specs & delivery info at the link" | "See the full specs on our site." |

## Example script — Two-tier under-sink organiser
1. HOOK: open cupboard under sink, bottles everywhere. Text: "Under-sink chaos?"
2. Slide frame in around the pipes. Text: "Fits around pipes — no drilling" *(only if the supplier confirms freestanding design and dimensions)*.
3. Pull baskets out. Text: "Baskets slide out".
4. Close door. Text: "Small spaces, sorted."
5. CTA: "Full measurements on our website."
