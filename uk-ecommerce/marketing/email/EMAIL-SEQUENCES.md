# Email sequences

Use **Shopify's native notifications and Shopify Email** (free allowance on Shopify plans; verify the current allowance). Shopify handles consent records and adds unsubscribe links automatically. Marketing emails go **only** to customers who opted in (newsletter checkbox or checkout opt-in).

## Transactional (Shopify → Settings → Notifications) — customise wording only
- **Order confirmation:** add: "We pass your order to our UK supplier, normally within 1 working day. You'll get tracking as soon as it's dispatched." Add a link to the returns policy.
- **Shipping confirmation:** the default includes tracking. Add: "Items from different suppliers may arrive separately."
- **Abandoned checkout** (Settings → Checkout → Abandoned checkouts → send automatically after 10 hours to subscribers only): one reminder, **no fake urgency or discounts**.

## Welcome sequence (Shopify Email automation; subscribers only)
1. **Immediately — "Welcome to Nook & Order"**: who we are (genuine), how delivery works, link to best-sellers. No discount unless you choose to offer one; if you do, state the terms honestly.
2. **Day 3 — "Measure twice, order once"**: measuring guide + 3 most useful products for small kitchens.
3. **Day 7 — "Renter-friendly fixes"**: no-drill product picks + returns reassurance (14-day cancellation).

## Post-purchase (Shopify Email automation; customers who opted in)
- **5 days after fulfilment — "How's it fitting?"**: care tips from the supplier; a link to contact us if anything is wrong. Do **not** ask only happy customers for reviews (review gating is misleading). If you add a review app later, ask everyone.

## Customer-service follow-ups
Handled by Workflow 08: verified answers for routine questions; neutral acknowledgement plus escalation to you for refunds, damage, complaints, chargebacks, legal or safety issues.
