# Brand concepts

Three identities for the small-space home organisation niche. **Nothing has been purchased or registered.** Name and domain availability is **NOT verified**: this environment could not query domain registrars or the UK IPO register. Do these checks yourself before committing (see bottom).

## A. Nook & Order — provisional preference ✅
- **Positioning:** calm, practical storage that makes small UK homes work, chosen to be renter-friendly and listed with honest measurements.
- **Audience:** 25–45-year-old renters and first-time buyers in flats and terraces; busy households short on cupboard space.
- **Tagline:** *Small spaces, sorted.*
- **Logo concept:** wordmark in a sturdy grotesque, with the ampersand drawn as a simple nested-box shape (two offset rounded rectangles). Monochrome-friendly; works as a favicon (the ampersand mark alone).
- **Colour palette:** Deep Fern `#2F5D50` (primary), Oat `#F7F4EE` (background), Linen `#ECE6DA` (surface), Clay `#C8664A` (highlight), Charcoal `#1F2421` (text), Sage Grey `#5B6560` (muted). Text/background contrast passes WCAG AA.
- **Typography:** Assistant (Shopify font library; bold for headings, regular for body). Fallback: system UI.
- **Website direction:** warm off-white pages, generous spacing, natural-light photography of real cupboards and hallways, measurements up front. No countdown timers, fake "x people viewing" or inflated discounts. *(This is what the built theme implements.)*
- **Suggested domains:** nookandorder.co.uk, nookandorder.com, nookandorder.uk (unverified)
- **Conflict check (web search, 12 Oct 2026):** no exact "Nook & Order" brand found. **Note:** *The Storage Nook Company* (Telford, under-stairs storage, trading since 2026) shares the word "Nook" in the same sector ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/thestoragenookcompany.co.uk)). Assess confusion risk with a trade-mark adviser before filing.

## B. Smallspace Supply Co.
- **Positioning:** no-nonsense, utilitarian fixes for flat-dwellers; "tools for tight spaces".
- **Audience:** urban renters, students, house-sharers.
- **Tagline:** *Room to breathe.*
- **Logo concept:** stencil-style wordmark inside a thin rectangle (like a storage label).
- **Palette:** Ink `#111111`, Paper `#FAFAF7`, Safety Yellow `#F2C230`, Concrete `#8E8E89`.
- **Typography:** a condensed grotesque for headings, a mono for specs.
- **Website direction:** grid layouts, spec-sheet product pages, bold dimensions.
- **Suggested domains:** smallspacesupply.co.uk / .com (unverified)
- **Conflict check:** no UK shop of that name found by web search. **Weakness:** the name is descriptive, so it is likely hard to register as a trade mark.

## C. Homekept
- **Positioning:** Scandinavian-calm, "a place for everything".
- **Audience:** homeowners decorating smaller homes; gift buyers.
- **Tagline:** *Everything in its place.*
- **Palette:** Birch `#F3EFE7`, Moss `#5E6B4E`, Rosewood `#9C5A4A`, Soot `#2A2A28`.
- **Typography:** soft humanist serif headings, clean sans body.
- **Website direction:** lifestyle-led imagery, room-by-room collections.
- **Suggested domains:** homekept.co.uk (unverified)
- **Conflict check:** a **US trade-mark application for HOMEKEPT in class 21** (household containers and utensils) was found ([Justia record](https://trademarks.justia.com/907/01/homekept-90701774.html)). Class 21 covers much of this product range. **Not recommended** without legal advice.

## Owner actions before choosing
1. Search the UK IPO trade-mark register (classes 20, 21, 35) for each name.
2. Check Companies House and social handles (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok).
3. Check domain availability at a registrar. **Approval needed before any purchase.**
4. Approve one identity. The theme's name and colours change in Shopify → Customise → Theme settings; no code change is needed.
