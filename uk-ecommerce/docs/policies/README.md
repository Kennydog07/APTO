These drafts reflect UK consumer law as understood in October 2026. Before publishing:
1. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
2. Have them reviewed by a UK solicitor or a reputable template service (several UK trade bodies offer member templates).
3. Paste them into Shopify → Settings → Policies (refund, privacy, terms, shipping, contact). Create pages for the cookie policy and complaints procedure, and link them in the footer menu.
