# Complaints procedure

_Last updated: [DATE]_

We want to put things right.

1. **Contact us** at [EMAIL] with your order number and what went wrong. Photos help.
2. **Acknowledgement:** we confirm receipt within 2 working days.
3. **Response:** we aim to give a full response within 10 working days. If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.
4. **Escalation:** if you are unhappy with our response, reply asking for a review. The business owner will personally review your complaint and reply within a further 10 working days.
5. **Further help:** you can get free advice from Citizens Advice consumer service (citizensadvice.org.uk, 0808 223 1133). You may also take your claim to court through the small claims process. We do not currently subscribe to an alternative dispute resolution (ADR) scheme. [OWNER: confirm or name an ADR provider.]

Every complaint is handled by a person, not an automated system.
