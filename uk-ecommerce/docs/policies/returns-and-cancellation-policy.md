# Returns and cancellation policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## Changed your mind? (14-day cancellation)
1. Tell us within **14 days of receiving your order**: email [EMAIL] with your order number and the item(s).
2. **Wait for our reply before posting anything.** Our products ship from different UK warehouses, so we will send you the correct return address.
3. Send the item back within 14 days of telling us. Please use a tracked service and keep proof of postage.
4. Return costs are paid by you unless the item is faulty, damaged or not as described.

Items should be returned in the condition you received them. You may handle them as you would in a shop. If an item has been used beyond that, we may reduce the refund to reflect any loss in value.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong item
Email [EMAIL] with your order number and photos. Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 include a full refund if you reject faulty goods within 30 days. We cover return costs for faulty or incorrect items.
