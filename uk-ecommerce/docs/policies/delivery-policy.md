# Delivery policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

- **Where we deliver:** addresses in Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales). We do not currently deliver to Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man or outside the UK.
- **How:** your order is sent from a UK warehouse operated by our supplier, using a tracked service.
- **When:** we normally pass your order to our supplier within 1 working day of payment. Each product page shows the supplier's delivery estimate. Estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.
- **Cost:** shown at checkout before you pay.
- **Tracking:** we email you the carrier and tracking number as soon as the supplier dispatches your order.
- **Multiple items:** items from different suppliers may arrive separately.
- **Late or missing parcels:** if your parcel has not arrived 3 working days after the estimate, contact [EMAIL] with your order number and we will investigate with the supplier and carrier. Goods remain our responsibility until they are delivered to you.
- **Wrong address:** contact us immediately. We can usually update the address if your order has not yet been passed to the supplier.
