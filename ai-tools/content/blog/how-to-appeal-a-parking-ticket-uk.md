Getting a parking ticket is stressful, but many are worth challenging. The first step is working out **what kind of ticket you have**, because the process is different.

## Council PCN or private parking charge?

- **Council Penalty Charge Notice (PCN):** issued by a local council, usually on streets or council car parks.
- **Private parking charge:** issued by a company that runs a car park, such as at a supermarket or retail park.

## What evidence helps

- Photos of unclear or missing signs
- Your pay-and-display ticket, permit or receipt
- Evidence a payment machine was broken
- Medical letters, if there was an emergency

## Write a firm, polite letter

Stick to the facts, explain your grounds clearly, and ask for the charge to be cancelled. Always **check the deadline printed on your ticket**.
