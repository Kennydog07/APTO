About Thai Dish

A little taste of Thailand in Bath Place

We are a small, independent restaurant tucked into Bath Place, the pretty lane between Worthing's town centre and the seafront. Our kitchen is all about fresh herbs, balanced spice and recipes cooked the traditional way.

  • Authentic recipes — curries, stir fries and noodle dishes with real Thai flavour.
  • Great value — and as we are BYO, a night out costs less.
  • Eat in or take away — call ahead and your order will be ready to collect.
