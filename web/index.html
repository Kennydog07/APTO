FAQ

Questions you might have

Why is it so cheap?

APTO is just getting started, so a select few local businesses get their website at £39.99. Soon the price goes back up to £79.99. It’s the same complete website either way, and care plan prices stay the same.

What do I get for £39.99?

A complete, simple website for your business, with both a front end and a back end. That means the pages your customers see (for example home, services, about and contact), a contact or enquiry form that actually works, and a basic admin back end. The £39.99 is a one-off, introductory price per website (normally £79.99).

Do I have to take a care plan?

No, care plans are optional. They’re there if you’d like us to keep your website looked after each year. Basic is £25 per year and Plus is £50 per year.

What’s the difference between Basic and Plus?

Basic (£25 per year) includes maintenance and hosting upkeep, plus 2 changes per year. Plus (£50 per year) includes maintenance and 5 changes per year, so it suits businesses that like to update their site more often.

What counts as a “change”?

A change is a small update to your existing site, such as editing some text, swapping a photo or updating your opening hours. If you’re not sure whether something counts as a change, just ask and we’ll let you know before doing anything.

What if I need more changes than my plan includes?

Get in touch and tell us what you need. We’ll talk it through with you and agree the details before any extra work is done.

Do I need to be good with computers?

Not at all. We handle the technical side and explain things in plain English. You just tell us about your business.

Can I use my own domain name?

Domain names (like yourbusiness.co.uk) are registered with a domain provider. If you already have one, or want one, mention it in your enquiry and we’ll talk you through the options.

How do I get started?

Fill in the enquiry form below with a few details about your business. We’ll get back to you to talk about what you need.
